# Android Game Ideas

## Mechanic-first concepts

1. **Gravity Painter** — You tilt the phone to control gravity direction, and paint drips across the screen filling shapes. Each level is a painting you complete by tilting.

2. **Echo Tap** — Tap a rhythm, and the game generates an enemy pattern based on it. Fight what you created. Every run is different because you set the pace.

3. **One Pixel** — You control a single pixel on screen. The challenge: the screen is covered in hazards, and you navigate purely by memory since the pixel is nearly invisible.

## Theme-first concepts

4. **Librarian** — Books fall off shelves. You slide them back in the right genre slot before the pile gets too high. Simple swipe mechanic, oddly satisfying.

5. **Signal Lost** — You're a radio tower sending pulses. Drag signal paths around obstacles (mountains, buildings) to reach receivers before the battery dies.

6. **Lichen** — A slow, meditative game where you grow lichen across a rock surface, competing with other organisms for territory. Turn-based, no timer.

## Twist on familiar genres

7. **Reverse Tower Defense** — You are the wave. Navigate your units through a maze of towers the AI placed, adapting your path in real time.

8. **Blind Platformer** — The screen is dark. Sound cues (footsteps, echoes, drips) tell you where platforms are. Short levels, high tension.

9. **Weight Puzzle** — Stack objects on a balance scale to hit an exact target weight. Objects have hidden variance — a "1kg rock" might be 0.9 or 1.1. You learn by feel over time.

---

**Best candidates for a solo dev:** Echo Tap, Librarian, Weight Puzzle — minimal assets, tight mechanics, easy to scope.
